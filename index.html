KiwiCue is a bilingual event discovery and reminder service helping people find concerts, markets, festivals, theatre, campus and community events in time to take part.

KiwiCue（纽村小报）是一项双语活动发现与提醒服务，帮助用户及时找到演唱会、集市、 节日、话剧、校园及社区活动。

01 / THE IDEA

One city. Many sources.
One clear view.

一座城市，多个来源，
一个清晰入口。

Useful event information is scattered across ticketing platforms, venues, public calendars, universities and organisers. KiwiCue brings the essentials into a timely, sortable view, then directs people to the official organiser or ticketing provider to register or buy tickets.

实用的活动信息分散在票务平台、场馆、公开日历、大学和主办方页面。KiwiCue 将关键内容整理成 及时、可筛选的信息入口，并引导用户前往主办方或官方票务页面完成报名和购票。

02 / THE STANDARD

Timely information.
Verifiable sources.

及时的信息，
可核实的来源。

  1. 01

    Official APIs, public calendars and organiser submissions come first.

    优先使用官方 API、公开日历和主办方投稿。

  2. 02

    Dates, venues, prices, deadlines and source links stay clear.

    清楚显示日期、场馆、价格、截止时间和来源。

  3. 03

    Cancellation, postponement and sold-out status deserve fast updates.

    及时更新取消、延期和售罄状态。

  4. 04

    KiwiCue does not sell tickets or process payments.

    KiwiCue 不销售门票，也不处理付款。

KiwiCue 纽村小报

KiwiCue is an independent project based in Auckland, New Zealand. Ticketing and registration remain with official providers. Not affiliated with any ticketing platform.
